A membership from start to finish

  1. Customer

    Buys a membership

    On the website, as now. They can buy several at once, one for each person.

  2. Customer

    Says who it's for

    At the checkout they pick a person on their account. If the person isn't listed, they choose "Someone not listed here".

  3. Your team

    Adds the person, if needed

    It appears under "Needs doing". You add the person and give them the membership. Their 12 months start on that day, so nobody loses any time.

  4. Customer

    Gets member prices

    Every class they book shows the member price for members and the normal price for everyone else.

The rules in plain words

12 months from the right day

Named at the checkout: 12 months from payment. Named later by your team: 12 months from that day.

Covers the day of the class

Members get the member price only if their membership still runs on the day of the class.

One discount at a time

The member price doesn't add on top of a sale price or a discount code.

Customers can't add people

New people are added by your team, as agreed. Customers choose "Someone not listed here".
